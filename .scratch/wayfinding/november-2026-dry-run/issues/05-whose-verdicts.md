# 05: Whose verdicts does the run use?

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 03

## Question

The organizer has already reviewed in the sheet: 165 Accepted, 10 In progress, 1 Dispute, 1 withdrew, 35 blank.
The proposal leaves that `Status` column out, and every imported application lands at `open`, so the product's review has to decide all of them again.

Options:

- **Replay the sheet**: approve the 165 Accepted, reject the withdrawn one, and decide the other 46 (In progress, Dispute, blank) by a stated rule. This is what makes a rehearsal's assignment comparable with reality.
- **Review fresh** on the cards, as an organizer would who had not used the sheet. Tests the review screen as the product intends it, but the result reflects the reviewer's guesses.

Either way it is 212 keyboard decisions through the page, as on the earlier runs.

Also: is it worth recording, as a usability finding, that an organizer who has already decided in the sheet has no way to bring those decisions in?

## Answer

Decided with the user on 2026-10-09: **replay the sheet, and the agent decides the rest as marked stand-ins.**

- The 165 rows marked Accepted are approved; the one marked "withdrew application" is rejected.
- The other 46 (10 In progress, 1 Dispute, 35 blank) are decided on the review cards by the agent, by the form's own stated rules: approve if UBC-affiliated (a UBC email given, or proof attached) and every certification ticked; reject if not UBC. Anything still unclear is listed for the user.
  The run report marks each of the 46 as a stand-in, not a real verdict.
- A "not UBC" answer is rejected on the card. The product has no way to say an answer disqualifies; that general design is its own map (see the map's Out of scope).
- Worth recording as a finding: an organizer who has already decided in the sheet cannot bring those decisions in, so the 165 are 165 keyboard approvals.

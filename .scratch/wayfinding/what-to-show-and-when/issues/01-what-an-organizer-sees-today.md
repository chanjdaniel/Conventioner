# 01: What an organizer sees today

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: -

## Question

For each intake path (form, CSV) and each phase a market of that path can reach, what does the organizer see: the page the market lands on, the rail's forward step and blockers, each tab's state, and what the screen leaves them unable to tell about what to do next?

Captured on an isolated stack, screenshots and a table in `walk/` beside this map.
The answer is the walk itself; tickets downstream cite it by file name.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-10-18: [walk/walk.md](../walk/walk.md), with 146 screenshots beside it.

The pattern under almost every finding: **the screens offer what the market cannot do yet, or no longer can, and say so only after the click.**
"Open Applications" on an empty draft, "Publish Market" before any run, "Send Offers" that can never succeed, "Reopen for Editing" with applications present; a preview grid under "No assignment yet"; Market Setup editable through market days with no word on what an edit affects.
The CSV path inherits the form path's words ("Open/Close Applications") for steps a vendor never sees, and first hears that its tiers have no tables two phases late, as a blocker listing 90 addresses.

Its "Visibly broken or wrong" list is plain fixes, which go straight to the backlog rather than through this map.

# 01: How rejections are shown today

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: -

## Question

Who sees a rejection, where, and when, on each intake path, and does a rejected vendor leak into anything downstream: the solver, the seats, the download, the door?

## Answer

Recorded in full in [`walk/research.md`](../walk/research.md), with the gaps ranked.
The two that mattered most:

- A rejected vendor could stay seated and check in.
  A verdict could change after a run, a hand placement accepted any address, and a pin for someone not approved was kept as an orphan that blocked nothing; going live needed only a stored run.
- The applicant's label gave a rule's rejection away before publication.
  Undecided read "Submitted" and every decided application read "Under Review", and the rules judge an application in the same request that saves it.

It fed the grilling in [02](02-who-sees-a-rejection-and-when.md).

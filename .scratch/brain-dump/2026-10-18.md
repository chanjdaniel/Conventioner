## Current issues

### Mission: the UI should say what to do, and when

-> E31 (map tickets [02](../wayfinding/what-to-show-and-when/issues/02-the-next-step-per-phase-and-path.md))

Many features work, but looking at the UI it is unclear to the organizer what to do and when.
The phases and the state tracking are right and should stay as they are.
What is missing is being strategic about what is shown, and when, given the market's state.

That should differ explicitly by how vendors reach the market:

- **In-house applications** - vendors apply on the market's own page (`intakeMode: form`).
- **Google Forms import** - the organizer imports a spreadsheet of application results (`intakeMode: csv`).

Each path should get a screen sequence tailored to it, not one shared sequence with the other path's steps left in.

### Market setup becomes immutable at a reasonable point in the flow

-> E31/F01

The market setup should stop being editable at some reasonable point in the flow.
That point is leaving draft (settled in round 1 of the grilling).

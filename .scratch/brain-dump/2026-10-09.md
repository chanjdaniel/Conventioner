### Market dates: each selected date is its own row, not a tag

-> E28/F01/S01 (reverses part of [the-plan-uses-its-space 02](../wayfinding/the-plan-uses-its-space/issues/02-market-dates-beside-their-calendar.md))

In Market Setup, the market dates card lists the selected dates on the right, beside the calendar.
They currently show as tags.
Each date should be its own row instead.

### Market dates: center the arrows in the month switcher

-> E28/F04/S02

In Market Setup, the arrows that switch the calendar to the previous or next month are not centered properly.
They should sit properly centered.

### View vendors gets the same filters as view tables

-> E28/F02/S02

In results, the view tables page can be filtered by date, section, tier, and full or half table.
The view vendors page should have the same filters.

### Results tables: put the "Change" text at the top right of the seat button

-> E28/F04/S01

In results, the seat buttons in the tables carry a "Change" hint.
That text should be aligned to the top right of the button.

### Results tables: the assigned, partial and empty counts filter the tables

-> E28/F02/S01

In results, the view tables page shows a count of tables by fill state, for example "301 assigned, 0 partial, 2 empty".
Each count should be a clickable button that quickly filters the tables down to the ones in that state.

### Market page links: center the "current" dot vertically

-> E28/F04/S02

Under the Assignment tab, the page links (Assignment, Result) mark the page the market's phase is on with a green dot beside the label.
On the Result link, the dot is not vertically centered against the text.
It should be.
The tabs in the market bar draw a similar dot, so check those too.

### Assignment priority: tidy the "Answers, best first" list

-> E28/F03/S01 (the whole rules table)

In the assignment priority rules, a rule on one of the organizer's form questions shows an "Answers, best first" list for ranking that question's answers.
It looks rough and needs an aesthetic cleanup: proper spacing, alignment, and so on.

## Outcome

The review's three options are Reject, Approve and **Needs follow-up**: a real status for an application that is not complete and needs someone to reach out to the applicant.
Someone is responsible for each follow-up.

## Why now

Brain dump 2026-10-19, an organizers' request: Skip records nothing, so an application waiting on the applicant looks like one nobody has opened.

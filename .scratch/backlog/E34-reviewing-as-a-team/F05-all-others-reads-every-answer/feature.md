## Outcome

In an assignment priority rule, a multi-select answer is placed by its best-placed choice, and a blank answer sorts last.
"All others" still means every answer the organizer did not place, all at equal priority.

## Why now

Brain dump 2026-10-19: checked on the real market's clubs question.
"All others" works as intended for one answer, but a multi-select answer is scored by the applicant's first choice only, and a blank answer joins "All others", so on the clubs rule an applicant who answered nothing ranked level with every club member.

## Stories

- `S01` - best choice, and blank last.

## Outcome

The Applications page has two views, One by one and All.
All lists every application with its status, giver and holder, filters by status (follow-ups and "Mine" included), and lets a reviewer tick applications and give one verdict to the selection.

## Why now

Brain dump 2026-10-19, an organizers' request; reverses real-market-readiness 04, as the epic explains.

- `S02` - a verdict on a selection.

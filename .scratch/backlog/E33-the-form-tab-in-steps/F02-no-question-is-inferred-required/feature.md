## Outcome

A question is required only when the organizer marks it so.
A form started from a Google Form's responses proposes every one of the organizer's questions as not required.

## Why now

Brain dump 2026-10-19: the proposal marks a question required when 97% of respondents answered it, and a required question that a row left blank skips that row at import, so the guess silently dropped real applicants.

## Stories

- `S01` - the proposal marks nothing required.

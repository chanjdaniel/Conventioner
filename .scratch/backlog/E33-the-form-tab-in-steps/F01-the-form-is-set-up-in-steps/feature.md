## Outcome

The Application Form tab is a sequence of steps with a step header and a clear Next: the essential questions (read-only), the organizer's own questions, the preview (online path only), and the review setup (what a reviewer reads first, and the automatic rejections).
Once the form locks, the tab opens on the review setup, which stays editable.

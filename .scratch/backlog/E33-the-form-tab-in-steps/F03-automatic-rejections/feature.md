## Outcome

On the review setup step, the organizer names questions and the answers to them that reject an application automatically.
An application that gives one arrives in review already rejected, with the reason, and a reviewer can overturn it.

## Why now

Brain dump 2026-10-19.
The real market rejects every applicant who does not agree to the no-show terms, or who is not a UBC student, alumnus or another accepted affiliation; today each is a manual rejection, one card at a time.

## Stories

- `S01` - the organizer sets the rules.
- `S02` - the rules reject, with a reason.

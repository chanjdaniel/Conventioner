## Outcome

An organizer can choose to take applications through a form on their market's public page, and can copy or open that page's link to send to vendors.

## Why now

The applicant surface is **built and switched off**, not withheld: the apply page, the applicant login, the applicant dashboard and their seeds all exist, and the apply page already answers correctly in every phase.
What is missing is any way for an organizer to turn it on, and any way for them to find the link.

Settled in [ticket 09](../../../wayfinding/the-order-of-the-work/issues/09-who-can-reach-the-public-application-url.md), which also retired the reason the control was withheld.

## The rule that does not change

Absence of an intake mode still means CSV, so the applicant surface keeps failing closed and no migration is needed.
Wrongly hiding an application surface is visible and gets complained about; wrongly exposing one is silent until a stranger applies.

## Stories

- `S01` - intake mode is an organizer choice.
- `S02` - the application chip.

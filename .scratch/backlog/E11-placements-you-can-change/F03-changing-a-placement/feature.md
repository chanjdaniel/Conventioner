## Outcome

An organizer can put a vendor in an empty seat, or trade two vendors' seats, from the screen that
shows which seats are free.

## Why now

The trigger for every change is a person - a vendor drops out, a vendor must move - but the question
every change asks is "where can they go", and only the Tables view can answer it.

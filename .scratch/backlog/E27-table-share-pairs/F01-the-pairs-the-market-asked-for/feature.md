## Outcome

A table-share request is read once, where the application is written, into the partner's address.
The solver pairs by the market's rule.
The organizer can see every request that pairs nobody, and why.

## Why now

See the epic.
The three stories follow the request's path: how it is written, how the solver reads it, and what the organizer sees.
`S02` and `S03` both read the address `S01` writes.

## Stories

- `S01` - the partner's address is read where the application is written.
- `S02` - the solver pairs by the market's rule.
- `S03` - a request that pairs nobody is shown.

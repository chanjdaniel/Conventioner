## Outcome

Captions line up with the controls they name, controls sit where their role says they should, and a button is set at the size the type scale names for buttons.

## Why now

The application form tab is the densest authoring surface in the product and three separate things on it are misaligned or mis-sized: the add-a-field editor's captions do not sit on their controls' centre line, the one toggle that decides whether a question is asked reads as a label in a heading row, and the field-type badge renders below the contrast bar.

Separately, the product's main action button is set two steps above what the type scale names for a button, in a rule shared by four buttons across three screens.

## Stories

- `S01` - the application form tab reads clearly.
- `S02` - buttons are button-sized.

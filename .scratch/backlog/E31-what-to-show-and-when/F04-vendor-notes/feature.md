## Outcome

A vendor can say what they need beyond their table, the organizer can record what they know and did about a vendor, and both are in front of whoever is looking at that vendor - in review, on the assignment's Vendors page, and at the door.

## Why now

Brain dump 2026-10-18; decided in [ticket 03](../../../wayfinding/what-to-show-and-when/issues/03-vendor-notes.md).
Two separate kinds, never one field with two authors.

## Stories

- `S01` - the vendor's request.
- `S02` - the organizer's note.
- `S03` - a sheet's organizer column comes in as "From your sheet".
- `S04` - notes where the vendor is looked at, and a request marked handled.

## Outcome

A form market and a CSV market each keep one set of tabs for their whole life, each tab live or read-only with a line saying when it matters; nothing on a CSV market suggests vendors apply there; each path offers only its own actions; and nothing is shown as assigned until an assignment is stored.

## Why now

[Ticket 02](../../../wayfinding/what-to-show-and-when/issues/02-the-next-step-per-phase-and-path.md) and the map's round 1.

## Stories

- `S01` - creating a market asks how vendors will apply.
- `S02` - each path keeps its tabs, live or read-only with a line.
- `S03` - the CSV path speaks of its Google Form.
- `S04` - each path offers only its own actions.
- `S05` - nothing is shown as assigned until a run is stored.

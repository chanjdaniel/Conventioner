## What to build

On a CSV market:

- The rail's stages and steps read "Start Collecting", "Collecting on your Google Form", "Done Collecting" and "Your Google Form is closed" where the form path reads "Open Applications", "Applications open", "Close Applications" and "Applications closed".
- The Application Form tab is "Questions" ("what your Google Form asks"), with no applicant preview: it is where the import's targets are defined.

The phases and their stored values do not change; only what is shown.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] No screen of a CSV market says vendors can apply, in any phase.
- [x] The form path's words are unchanged.

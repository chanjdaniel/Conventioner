## What to build

Once the market's last date has passed, "Archive Market" is the rail's forward step, through its existing permanent-action dialog, and the statement says the market has run and archiving keeps it as a record.
Before then it stays in the menu, and during market days the statement is "Check vendors in at the door" (Attendance).
The last date is compared as a calendar day, like every market date.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On market days before the last date, no forward step; after it, Archive is.
- [ ] The comparison holds in Honolulu, Los Angeles and Tokyo.

## Outcome

On every market screen the rail states the one next step for the market's phase and path, links to the page where it is done, and shows the forward step disabled with its first blocker when the server would refuse it.

## Why now

[Ticket 02](../../../wayfinding/what-to-show-and-when/issues/02-the-next-step-per-phase-and-path.md): the walk found "Open Applications" offered on an empty draft, "Publish Market" before any run and "Reopen for Editing" with applications present, each refused only after the click.

## Stories

- `S01` - the market read serves each move's verdict.
- `S02` - the rail states the next step, from one table.
- `S03` - archiving is the next step once the market has run.

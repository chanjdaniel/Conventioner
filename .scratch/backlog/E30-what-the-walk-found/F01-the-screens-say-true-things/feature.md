## Outcome

Every market page loads in every phase, and its words, links and menu items describe the phase the market is actually in.

## Why now

The [walk](../../../wayfinding/what-to-show-and-when/walk/walk.md)'s "Visibly broken or wrong" list.

## Stories

- `S01` - a fresh draft's Result and Vendors load.
- `S02` - no message describes a phase the market has left.
- `S03` - the rail's links read in full and point at the right page.
- `S04` - the menu offers nothing that can never succeed.
- `S05` - lists read in the order the work happens.

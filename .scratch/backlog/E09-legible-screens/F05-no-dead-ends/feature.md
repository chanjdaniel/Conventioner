## Outcome

Every page the organizer can reach has a way onward, no button navigates to a page that only says no, and no screen renders an editable wizard attached to no market.

## Why now

The import wizard has no exit at any of its four steps.
The 404 has no link home.
The public check-in page renders a hamburger that opens nothing.
And `/market-setup` after a fresh sign-in renders a page titled "Settings" with an empty Market Dates panel and a live Next button.

## Outcome

No screen renders content outside the box that is supposed to hold it, no screen has more than one thing that scrolls, and no label breaks in the middle of a word.

## Why now

The Tables view - the screen an organizer reads at the door on market day - shows six of twenty-four tables and there is no scrollbar anywhere that reaches the rest.
It is the same shape of bug as the Applications tab, which `E08/F01/S03` fixed as a single instance.

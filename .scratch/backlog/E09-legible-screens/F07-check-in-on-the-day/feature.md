## Outcome

A vendor at the door knows they are at the right market's page before they type anything, and cannot check in for the wrong day.

## Why now

The public check-in page is the product's only vendor-facing surface and the one place a stranger meets it.
It works well on a phone - `E08/F03` delivered that - but it does not name the market until after a lookup, and it offers an identical "Check in" button for every market date with no indication which one is today.

## Outcome

One concept has one name, one date has one format, and no hint describes a state the product is no longer in.

## Why now

A market date is written five different ways across six screens, and the one in the editor where you type it omits the year.
A table an applicant asked for is "A whole table to myself" on the form, "Full Table" on the payoff screen and `full` in storage.
The triage panel still advises adding a custom field "while the market is a draft and nobody has applied" after five people have applied and the form is locked.

## Outcome

Every control an organizer has to find looks like something you can operate, is reachable by keyboard, and is the size of the thing it claims to be.

## Why now

The rows an organizer builds every tier, location and section with carry a delete icon squashed from 24x24 to 8x20, a drag handle painted outside its own box, and dropdowns that render completely blank.
The primary action of the create-market dialog is bare text with no background, no border and no padding, identical enabled and disabled.
None of this is subtle once seen, and all of it is on the first screens of a new market.

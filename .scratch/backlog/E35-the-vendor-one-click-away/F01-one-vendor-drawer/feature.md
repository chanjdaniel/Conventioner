## Outcome

One vendor drawer, with tabs (Application, Assignments, History) and the vendor's notes pinned above them, opens from the Vendors list, from a seat, from an attendance entry and from the bulk review.

## Why now

Brain dump 2026-10-19: the notes and the submission sit well, but assignments and placement history are at the bottom where they are hard to reach, and the drawer opens only from the Vendors page.

## Stories

- `S01` - the drawer has tabs.
- `S02` - a seat and an attendance entry open it.

## Outcome

A walk-in or a late arrangement on the day is recorded as a vendor the market knows: a name, an address, who added them, and a seat placed by hand.

## Why now

The only way to seat someone who never applied was to type an address into a seat through the API, which `F02/S01` closes.
Decision 7, from Daniel's "additions are done by hand during market days".

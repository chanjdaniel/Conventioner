## Outcome

Before results are published, every applicant reads "Submitted"; after, exactly one of "You have a table" or "You did not get assigned", and never a reason.

## Why now

Undecided read "Submitted" and every decided application "Under Review", and the rules judge an application as it is saved, so an applicant learned a rule had rejected them the moment they submitted (research 6.2).
After publishing, the page showed a verdict word, which a hand placement or a vendor added on the day can contradict.
Decisions 12-13.

- `S01` - "Submitted" until results are published.
- `S02` - two outcomes after.

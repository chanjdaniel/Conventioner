## Outcome

On a Google Form market, the organizer walks Plan, Import, Review, Assign, Market days.
They are never asked to start or finish "collecting": they already hold the responses, so the upload is the collection.
Review is a distinct step from importing, and the review offers no import.

## Why now

Brain dump 2026-10-19: Start Collecting led nowhere, Done Collecting was offered with nothing imported, and Import from CSV was still offered in review, where the server refuses it.

## Stories

- `S01` - the path's stages are Plan, Import, Review.
- `S02` - a late export comes in from the menu.
- `S03` - the review offers no import.

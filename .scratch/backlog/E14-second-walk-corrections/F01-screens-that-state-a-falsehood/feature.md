## Outcome

Three screens stop saying things that are not true: the dashboard about whether you have a market, every applicant-facing date about its year, and a blocker about where its fix lives.

## Why now

None of these carries a decision - the cause is known and the fix is a line or two in each case - but all three are visible on first contact, and the date bug is on the screen where the organizer decides who gets a table.

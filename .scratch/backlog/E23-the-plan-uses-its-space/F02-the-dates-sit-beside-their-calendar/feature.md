## Outcome

The market dates card is a calendar on the left and the chosen dates on the right, grouped by month, using the card's width rather than leaving it empty.

## Why now

Decided in [the-plan-uses-its-space 02](../../../wayfinding/the-plan-uses-its-space/issues/02-market-dates-beside-their-calendar.md), from a prototype of three lists at 1, 5 and 20 dates.

## Stories

- `S01` - the chosen dates are listed by month beside the calendar.

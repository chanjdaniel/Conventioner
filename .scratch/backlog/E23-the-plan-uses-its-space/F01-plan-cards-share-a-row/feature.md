## Outcome

A screen of cards is laid out by one written rule, and the plan follows it: Tier Setup beside Location Setup, How vendors apply beside Application form, Market Dates and Section Setup at full width.

## Why now

Decided in [the-plan-uses-its-space 01](../../../wayfinding/the-plan-uses-its-space/issues/01-the-row-rule.md), from a prototype of three rules on the real plan.
The design system names two screen widths but says nothing about how cards share a row, which is why every plan card has a row to itself.

## Stories

- `S01` - the row rule is written, and the plan follows it.

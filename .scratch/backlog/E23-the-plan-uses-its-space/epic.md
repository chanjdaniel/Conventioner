## Outcome

The plan screen reads at 1920x1080 without stranded width: every card is sized to what it holds and shares a row when it fits, by one written rule in `docs/design-system.md`.
The market dates sit as a calendar beside the list of chosen dates, and Tier Setup sits beside Location Setup.

## Why now

Two findings from the 2026-09-26 brain dump: the market dates card leaves most of its width empty, and Tier Setup and Location Setup are each far wider than the little they hold.

Charted in [the-plan-uses-its-space](../../wayfinding/the-plan-uses-its-space/map.md).
The map is closed: every question is resolved, and the two features below are the whole of the work.

## Features

- `F01` - plan cards share a row, by one written rule. Startable now.
- `F02` - the market dates sit beside their calendar, listed by month. Startable now.

## Order inside this epic

The two features are independent; `F01` first is easier to review, since it settles the card's width.

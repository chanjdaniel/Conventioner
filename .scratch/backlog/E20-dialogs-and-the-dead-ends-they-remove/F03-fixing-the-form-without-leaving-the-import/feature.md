## Outcome

When the column mapping reveals the form asked the wrong thing, the organizer fixes it without losing their upload.

## Why now

The import wizard has the worst dead end in the product attached to the best writing in it.
Today it prints four manual steps and two phase transitions, and the organizer loses their file and their partial mapping on the way.

The constraint behind that is honest and is written down at the point of pain: changing the form is only legal while the market is a draft, and the wizard never runs in one.
This feature removes the dead end rather than rediscovering it.

Settled in [ticket 07](../../../wayfinding/the-order-of-the-work/issues/07-editing-the-form-from-the-import-wizard.md).

## Outcome

No organizer screen shows a value meant for the database, every market screen names the market it is showing, and the check-in page's primary action looks like one.

## Why now

Four small things, each visible on a screen an organizer uses in earnest: a reviewer deciding 232 applications reads `true` as an answer; the form-lock banner says `market_days` two inches below a rail saying "Market Days"; two of the four market screens do not name their market at all; and the page whose whole purpose is checking in renders `Check in` as its quietest control.

None carries a decision.

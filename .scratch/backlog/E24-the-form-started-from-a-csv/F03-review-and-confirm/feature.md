## Outcome

The organizer reviews the proposal in one ledger, in their file's order, changes what is wrong, and confirms once; the plan facts, the form, the ceiling and the import mapping are written together, and the first import of that form's responses opens already mapped.

## Why now

The proposal is guesses, and a guess the organizer cannot see is worse than none.
Settled in [05](../../../wayfinding/the-form-started-from-a-csv/issues/05-how-you-review-what-the-csv-proposes.md) (behaviour, and the layout from the prototype on `prototype/csv-proposal`, variant A) and [06](../../../wayfinding/the-form-started-from-a-csv/issues/06-one-answer-two-uses.md) (what confirm writes).

## Stories

- `S01` - the proposal ledger.
- `S02` - the organizer corrects the proposal.
- `S03` - one confirm writes it all.

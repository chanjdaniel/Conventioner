## Outcome

Five realistic Google Form exports are committed, with nothing in them that identifies anyone, and every test in this epic reads them.

## Why now

The five real exports are the only realistic responses there are, and they cannot be committed: they carry vendors' names, emails, handles, Drive links and organizer notes.
The rules were measured on them ([03](../../../wayfinding/the-form-started-from-a-csv/issues/03-how-good-are-the-rules-alone.md)); without committed copies nothing can hold the rules to those numbers.
Method settled in [07](../../../wayfinding/the-form-started-from-a-csv/issues/07-anonymise-the-corpus.md).

## Outcome

An organizer can start a market from their Google Form when they create it, and from a draft's Market Setup while its form has no questions of its own.

## Why now

The flow needs a way in; settled in [05](../../../wayfinding/the-form-started-from-a-csv/issues/05-how-you-review-what-the-csv-proposes.md).
Only the creation path sets the intake mode, because only there has the organizer said how vendors reach this market; the Market Setup action leaves it alone, which is how an organizer whose vendors apply on this market's page can seed this year's form from last year's responses at no extra cost.

## Stories

- `S01` - "I already have a Google Form" at creation.
- `S02` - start from a draft's Market Setup.

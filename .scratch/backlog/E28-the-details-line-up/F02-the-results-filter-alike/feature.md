## Outcome

On the tables page, the assigned, partial and empty counts are filters as well as counts.
The vendors page has the tables page's date, section, tier and table filters, from the same filter bar and under the same address keys, so a filter survives moving between the two pages.

## Why now

Brain dump 2026-10-09.
The vendors page could only be searched by name or address, on a market of 211 vendors across three dates.

## Stories

- `S01` - the table counts filter the tables.
- `S02` - the vendors page filters as the tables page does.

## Outcome

An organizer with many markets finds the one they want on the Markets page by typing part of its name, narrowing by organization and phase, and choosing an order, and the page remembers what they chose when they come back to it.

## Why now

From the 2026-09-30 request: the Markets page lists every market in no particular order - the server applies none and neither does the page - and there is no way to narrow it.
It worked while an organizer had a handful; it stops working as they accumulate, since archived markets never leave the list.

Not charted as a Wayfinder map: every question was settled in one grilling round on 2026-09-30, recorded in the story.

## Features

- `F01` - search, sort and filter on the Markets page.

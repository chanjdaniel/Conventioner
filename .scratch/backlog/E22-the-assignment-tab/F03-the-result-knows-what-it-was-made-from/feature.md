## Outcome

When anything the solver reads has changed since the assignment was run, the result says so, and names what changed.

## Why now

Decided in [the-assignment-tab 03](../../../wayfinding/the-assignment-tab/issues/03-does-a-result-know-its-rules-changed.md).
Editing the rules, the plan or the approved applications never changes a stored assignment - only running it again does.
With the rules and the result on separate pages, an organizer can change a rule and read an assignment made under the old one without being told.

## Stories

- `S01` - a run records what it was made from, and the market says when that has changed.
- `S02` - the result says it is out of date.

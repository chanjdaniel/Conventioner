## Outcome

A review card leads with the answers that decide it.

## Why now

The card renders every answer an application carries in one flat list.
The product already believes some answers matter more - it orders the organizer's own questions before the essential ones, and the reasoning is written down: they "are what distinguish applicants from each other".
But it **infers** that from who authored the field, and the inference is wrong in both directions: a custom "how did you hear about us?" is noise on a triage card, and an essential availability answer can be the whole decision.

Settled in [ticket 05](../../../wayfinding/the-order-of-the-work/issues/05-which-answers-matter-for-review.md).

## Why it lives on the market and not on the form

The freeze decides it.
The application form locks at the first application, and **an organizer learns which answers they needed while reviewing** - which is after that moment.
A flag on a form field would freeze exactly when it becomes knowable, and could never mark the essential answers, which are not form fields at all.

## Stories

- `S01` - review highlights lead the card.
- `S02` - highlights can be changed while reviewing.

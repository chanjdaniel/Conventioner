## Outcome

Every vendor is called by the name they chose.

## Why now

The committed export carries **Full Legal Name** and **Preferred Name** as adjacent columns, and the ticket that gave names a home said outright that the product "drops both because it has nowhere to put them" - then built a home for the legal name only.

What that ticket explicitly deferred was a *trading name* - a business name rather than a person's - which is a different thing and does not cover this.
So this is a gap, not a reopened decision: the fixture has the data, the contract has no key for it, and the product calls people by their legal name.

Settled in [ticket 04](../../../wayfinding/the-order-of-the-work/issues/04-what-the-form-asks-and-in-how-many-shapes.md).

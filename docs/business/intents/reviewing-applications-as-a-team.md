# Reviewing applications as a team

A team of organizers reviews one market's applications at the same time, without two of them deciding the same application or one overwriting another's verdict.
Every verdict says who gave it; each card leads with the answers that decide it; the clear-cut cases arrive already rejected, with the reason, for an Admin or the owner to overturn if wrong; a verdict once given is changed only by an Admin or the owner; and an application that cannot be decided until someone reaches the vendor waits as a follow-up, with someone responsible for it.
A reviewer works one by one, or on a selection they tick in a view of every application.
The review is finished before the assignment begins, and from then on its verdicts settle.

## Why

The organizers of a real market review together, over hundreds of applications, so a queue held in each reviewer's browser and a verdict that records nobody sends two people to the same card and loses the work of one.
An organizer learns which answers decide an application by reading real ones, so what leads the card must stay changeable after the form has locked.
The rejections a market always makes, such as an applicant who did not agree to its terms, are a rule rather than a judgement, and should not cost a reviewer a card each.
A verdict that could still change after a run let a rejected vendor keep their seat into market day, so the review closes when the assignment begins, and a later correction is a seat changed by hand.

## Rules

- [Verdicts settle on entering assignment](../rules/verdicts-settle-on-entering-assignment.md): verdicts are open while the market can reach review, and closed after.
- [Needs follow-up](../rules/needs-follow-up.md): an application waiting on someone, with an owner, blocking the assignment.
- [Automatic rejections](../rules/automatic-rejections.md): answers the organizer names reject an undecided application, with the reason.
- [Review highlights](../rules/review-highlights.md): the answers a reviewer reads first, marked on the market and changeable mid-queue.
- [Vendor notes](../rules/vendor-notes.md): the vendor's request and the organizer's note, kept apart.
- [A vendor is called by their chosen name](../rules/a-vendor-is-called-by-their-chosen-name.md): every card and list names a vendor as they chose.

## Flows

- [Reviewing applications](../flows/review.md): one by one and All, follow-ups, and two reviewers at once.
- [Building the application form](../flows/form-tab.md): the review setup, where the highlights and the automatic rejections are set.

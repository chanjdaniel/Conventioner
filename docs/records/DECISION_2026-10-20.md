# Decision of 2026-10-20: an organization's admins run its markets

Daniel's ruling of 2026-10-20, made in the November 2026 market's window, which a node cites as `decision/2026-10-20`.
It was first recorded only in the message and the bug ledger entries of commit `84ccd19f`, on a branch that never shipped and is deleted once T15 merges; this record keeps it.
T15 rebuilt that commit on `dev` ([#121](https://github.com/chanjdaniel/Conventioner/pull/121)).

## What was decided

- An organization's owner and admins are Admin on every market the organization holds, and a member is Viewer.
- A role on the market itself is held beside the organization's, and the higher of the two counts, in every permission check and in the Markets list alike.
- Nobody is a market's Owner through its organization: a market's one Owner alone deletes it and hands it over.

## Why

The admin running a market on the day opened Result and could read every seat but swap none.
Membership in an organization gave Viewer on its markets whatever the role in it, and the Markets list called the organization's admin a viewer and offered no Manage.
A role given on the market itself replaced the organization's instead of adding to it, so an admin once given Viewer on one market was held to Viewer there.
Giving each admin a role on each market by hand was a step nobody knew to take.

## What it reversed

"Membership gives Viewer only", written on 2026-10-18 in the operations runbook (`a9b2f254`) while documenting production.
That line set down what the code already did, so an operator would know to give a door volunteer a role on the market; no backlog item, Wayfinder ticket or brain dump had chosen it.

The rule as it stands now is [what each role may do](../business/rules/what-each-role-may-do.md).

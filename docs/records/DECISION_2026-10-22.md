# Decision of 2026-10-22: who is offered a follow-up's owners

Daniel's ruling of 2026-10-22, on the review of T15 ([#121](https://github.com/chanjdaniel/Conventioner/pull/121)), which a node cites as `decision/2026-10-22`.

## What was decided

- Someone in the market's organization - its owner, an admin or a member - who may give verdicts is offered every person with a role on the market as a follow-up's owner: the organization's people and the people the market's roles name.
- Someone outside the organization, at Editor or above by a role on the market itself, is offered only the people the market's roles name, and no other organization address.
- A Viewer is offered nobody, as before.
- The server takes as a follow-up's owner exactly the people that requester is offered, so a request written by hand reaches no one else.

## Why

T15 let a follow-up be owned by anyone its organization reaches, and served those people's addresses to every Editor.
An Editor from outside the organization - a volunteer given a role on one market - would read the address of everyone in the organization, members who have nothing to do with that market included.
Of the options the review put to Daniel, keeping the full list, narrowing it for everyone, or narrowing it for whoever is outside the organization, they chose the last.

The rule as it stands now is [needs follow-up](../business/rules/needs-follow-up.md#who-may).
